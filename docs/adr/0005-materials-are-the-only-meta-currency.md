# Materials are the only meta currency

Jack's original pitch included soul energy as a second meta currency. We decided Materials are the only thing that Extracts and spends in the Soul World — XP stays in-run, soul energy is not a currency. Every Planet drops Echolith for generic Skill Tree stats, plus one or two Signature Materials that gate that Planet's branch, Buildings, and Decorations. One wallet, three sinks (tree, Buildings, Decorations). A second currency would split the extraction fantasy and make Salvage bookkeeping worse.
