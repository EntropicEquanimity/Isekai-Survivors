# Offers are weighted by Affinity, not class-locked and not a shared pile

A fully shared weapon pool (every Vessel sees the same Offers) and hard class loot were both on the table. We decided Vessels, Weapons, and Items have Affinities. Offer chance scales with overlap — Rogue is Crit, Shadow, Blade, Dodge — so a body leans into a feel without a sealed pool. Weapons and Items are XP that Jack's Soul shaped during the Run; they dissolve at Extraction. The lasting path is Materials and the Skill Tree.
