# The Soul World room is the menu

Hades-style hubs usually mean NPCs, shops, and a courtyard. We decided the Soul World is Jack's expanding room and that Buildings in it are the UI: Heart, Wardrobe, Orrery, Reliquary. No hub NPCs. Unlocking Skill Tree sections pushes the walls out. That keeps the Soul personal — you are upgrading a being, not visiting a town — and avoids standing up a cast before the in-run game is done.
